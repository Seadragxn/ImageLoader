Load PNG and JPEG images from web URLs, convert them to vanilla or exact RGB blocks, preview the result, and place it anywhere in the world.

Press P by default to open Image Loader. The key can be changed in Controls.

Image placement is server-authoritative in multiplayer and works in Classic, Expert, Master, and Journey worlds. Exact RGB blocks can be broken, carried with their colour value, and placed again. Gallery Mode adds bright noclip viewing and zoom-in controls.
