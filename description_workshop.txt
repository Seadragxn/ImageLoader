Load PNG and JPEG images from web URLs, convert their pixels to the closest Terraria solid-block colours, preview the result, and place it anywhere in the world.

Press P by default to open Image Loader. The key can be changed in Controls.

Image placement is server-authoritative in multiplayer and works in Classic, Expert, Master, and Journey worlds.
