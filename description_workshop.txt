Load PNG and JPEG images from web URLs and place them as Vanilla Blocks or Exact RGB pixels.

Press P by default to open Image Loader. The key can be changed in Controls.

Includes transparent-image support, aspect-ratio controls, breakable colour-carrying RGB blocks, fullbright mosaics, map/minimap RGB colour, server-authoritative multiplayer placement, bright Gallery noclip and zoom-in, and optional Void Gallery world generation.

Press P to open Image Loader or use /imageloader. Press G to toggle Gallery Mode.

Works in Classic, Expert, Master, and Journey worlds.
